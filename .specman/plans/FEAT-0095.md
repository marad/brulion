Approach: Make `goal` a concise execution wrapper around the repository protocol:
  preflight first, then one phase at a time, with durable state and explicit

## AC-4 (added): Given the code-review and review-until-clean skills are invoked, when a
  diff is reviewed, then the instructions require discriminating tests for
  fixes, restructuring after two rounds of the same finding class, and a
  clean stopping condition; they do not authorize silently treating a timed
  out mutation worker as successful.

Approach: Define review as an adversarial, diff-scoped pass and make the loop
  explicit: record findings, fix only owned changes, rerun until clean, and
  restructure recurring root causes. A stopped worker is never treated as a
  successful review result.

Files:
- `.pi/skills/code-review/SKILL.md`
- `.pi/skills/review-until-clean/SKILL.md`
